Catima file and link formats (credit)

Tillcard reads and writes the import/export format and the share-link format of Catima, the free and open-source
loyalty card wallet for Android by Sylvia van Os and contributors (https://github.com/CatimaLoyalty/Android, GPL-3.0).
Tillcard implements the documented formats itself, contains no Catima code, and is not affiliated with or endorsed by
the Catima project. Thank you to the Catima team for keeping the cards of their users portable.
